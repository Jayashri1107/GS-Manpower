Hospital housekeeping

Clean, safe patient areas maintained by staff who understand a hospital is not an ordinary building.

Scope

  • Wards, ICUs, OPD, corridors, washrooms and common areas
  • Daily, terminal and spill cleaning to your infection-control schedule
  • Segregation and internal movement of bio-medical waste by colour code
  • Linen movement and pantry support

Training before deployment

Hand hygiene, use of personal protective equipment, dilution of disinfectants, colour-coded waste segregation and patient privacy.

Patient attendants (non-clinical)

Respectful, physical and practical support for patients and ward teams.

Scope

  • Patient transfer by wheelchair and stretcher, and help with walking
  • Feeding assistance and personal hygiene support under ward instructions
  • Sample, file and medicine-bag runs between departments
  • Bedside companionship and call-bell response

What our attendants do not do. They are not nurses and do not give injections or medicines, change dressings, handle IV lines or make any clinical decision. All patient-care tasks are carried out under the direction of your nursing staff.

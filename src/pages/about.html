About GSMS

A local manpower partner that stays accountable

We provide institutions in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar with dependable staff, and we treat the people we place with the same care we promise our clients.

Who we are

Built around the needs of hospitals, offices and industry

GS Manpower Services was set up to give institutions in Marathwada a staffing partner that is easy to reach, careful about who it places, and transparent about costs.

We focus on a few service lines and do them properly: housekeeping, non-clinical patient support, office administration, industrial workforce and recruitment. Our team is based in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, close to the hospitals, campuses and MIDC areas we serve.

Our mission

To give every institution we serve reliable, well-trained people, and to give every worker we place fair pay, safe conditions and a path to grow.

How we work

Clear proposals, a single point of contact, monthly reviews and documents you can audit. If something is not working, we fix it quickly.

Our values

What you can expect from us

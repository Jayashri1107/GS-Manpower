About GS Manpower

No worker sent out alone

Our mark is a navy G around a saffron worker. The company surrounds, protects and stands behind every person it places. That is why clients trust a GS badge on site.

Who we are

Built around the needs of hospitals, offices and industry

GS Manpower Services was set up to give institutions in Marathwada a staffing partner that is easy to reach, careful about who it places, and transparent about costs.

We work through two divisions. GS Care supplies nurses, GDAs, technicians, housekeeping and front-office staff to hospitals. GS Industrial supplies operators, helpers, packers and contract labour to plants in Waluj, Shendra and other MIDC areas. Our team is based in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, close to the sites we serve.

Our mission

To give every institution we serve reliable, well-trained people, and to give every worker we place fair pay, safe conditions and a path to grow.

How we work

Clear proposals, a single point of contact, monthly reviews and documents you can audit. If something is not working, we fix it quickly.

Our values

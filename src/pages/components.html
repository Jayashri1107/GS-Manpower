Colour

Proportion: 60 navy · 25 paper/white · 5 steel · 10 saffron. Saffron is a signal, not a background: the centre figure and one call to action per layout. Never saffron text on white or paper.

Voice

Say: "12 trained GDAs, deployed in 72 hours." "Joining Monday, 7 AM, Gate 3."

Don't say: "Best manpower solutions in Maharashtra." No "best", "No. 1" or "world-class".

Plain, punctual, provable. Client-facing pages lead in English; worker-facing pages lead in Marathi, with Hindi for migrant workers in MIDC.

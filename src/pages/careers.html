How to apply

Four simple steps

  1. Apply online or call

    Fill in the form below, send a WhatsApp message, or visit our office.

  2. Talk to our team

    A recruiter calls you to understand your experience and preferred location.

  3. Interview and checks

    Bring original ID and address proof to the interview for verification.

  4. Training and joining

    Complete a short induction, receive your joining letter and start work.

What you get

Working with GSMS

  • Joining letter and clear wage details before you start
  • Monthly salary paid to your bank account
  • EPF and ESIC benefits where applicable
  • Uniform and safety equipment for your role
  • Training that helps you move to better roles

Keep these ready for the interview

Original identity proof, address proof, a passport-size photo, and experience letters if you have them. Please do not send identity documents online or over WhatsApp.

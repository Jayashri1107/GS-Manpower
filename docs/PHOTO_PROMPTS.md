# Images: illustrations now, photos later

The site currently uses **brand illustrations** (flat vector art in navy, saffron and teal), generated by
`scripts/illustrations.js` into `src/assets/img/illustrations/`. They replace every grey placeholder and are honest:
nobody mistakes them for real staff.

## If you want AI-generated photos

Use an image tool (for example Adobe Firefly, Ideogram, Midjourney or ChatGPT image generation) with the prompts below.
Before publishing an AI photo:
- Make sure the tool's licence allows commercial use.
- Do not present AI people as your real staff or clients. Captions such as "Our team at Apollo Hospital" are not
  allowed on AI images (Brand Guidelines p.09: "Promise only what we keep"). Neutral alt text is fine.
- Avoid real hospital or company logos, and never show identifiable patients.
- Best long-term choice: a half-day photo shoot with your real staff (signed model releases).

## Prompts (one per image on the site)

Common style line – add to every prompt:
> Documentary-style photograph, natural daylight, realistic Indian workplace in Maharashtra, people in clean uniforms,
> calm and professional, muted colours with navy blue and warm saffron accents, no text, no logos, 35 mm lens, shallow depth of field.

## How to swap an illustration for a photo

1. Export the photo as **WebP** (quality about 75) at the size in the table; keep it under 250 KB (hero under 150 KB).
2. Save it in `src/assets/img/photos/`, for example `hero-care.webp`.
3. In the page file (English in `src/pages/`, Marathi in `src/pages/mr/`), change the image path in the `<img>` tag from
   `assets/img/illustrations/hero-care.svg` to `assets/img/photos/hero-care.webp`, and update the `alt` text and the
   `width`/`height` to the photo's real size.
4. Save; `npm run dev` rebuilds. Check the page at phone and desktop size.
